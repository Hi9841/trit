# Commercial licence

Trit is open source under the AGPL-3.0. A company that uses Trit in a
commercial product or a paid service must get commercial rights in one of
these ways:

1. **Buy a commercial licence** (the terms below).
2. **Pay for a custom agreement:** support, a feature, or an exclusive
   term.
3. **Acquire or hire the author** (acqui-hire), with the rights to Trit.

The commercial licence lets you use Trit without the conditions of the
AGPL-3.0.

## What the licence covers

- Use of Trit (the `trit` library, the `trit` CLI, `trit-ffi`, and the
  bindings) in a closed-source product, device, or SDK.
- Use of Trit in a paid or hosted service (SaaS, an API), with no
  obligation to publish your source code.
- Changes to Trit that you keep private.
- Distribution of Trit in binary form inside your product.

## What the licence does not cover

- The models. Each model has its own licence. Some models forbid
  commercial use (refer to [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)).
- Third-party code and data in Trit. Their licences continue to apply
  (refer to [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)).

The terms, the price, and support are agreed in a written contract for
each licensee.
